HOROLOGICAL RIGOR

CRAFTED BY HAND.
MEASURED IN DECADES.

Every APEX watch originates within our private atelier in C-Scheme, Jaipur. In an era of automated micro-assembly, our watchmakers preserve centuries-old hand-chamfering, guilloché engraving, and chronometer regulation.

Hand Finishing & Chamfering 40+ HOURS

Every bridge undergoes traditional black polishing using gentian wood pegs and diamond paste. Micro-bevels are angled by eye without CNC assistance for luminous reflection.

Proven Calibres +/- 2 SEC / DAY

High-beat mechanical escapements regulated across five spatial positions and three temperature differentials in Jaipur.

316L Solid Marine Steel CORROSION PROOF

Monobloc case architectures cold-forged and faceted with alternating mirror and satin brushed lines.

JANTAR MANTAR LEGACY

BORN IN JAIPUR.
FOR THE WORLD.

In 1734, Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II erected the world’s most formidable stone astronomical observatories in Jaipur to measure the march of celestial time. APEX channels this three-century heritage of cosmic observation into mechanical miniaturization worn upon the wrist.
