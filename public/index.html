Since 1894

Born in Geneva,
Mastered for Eternity

Founded in 1894 along the shores of Lake Geneva by master horologist Henri de L’Apex, our manufacture has remained staunchly independent for over a century. We answer solely to our craft.

"We do not merely measure time. We sculpt the seconds into enduring physical monuments."

— Laurent de L’Apex, Master Watchmaker
  • 1894

    Founding of the Atelier

    Henri de L’Apex registers his initial patent for an ultra-flat pocket watch tourbillon in Geneva, winning the Grand Prix de Chronométrie.

  • 1952

    The Meridian Calibre Breakthrough

    Creation of the legendary high-beat automatic movement with patented bi-directional ceramic ball bearings.

  • 2008

    The Silicon Escapement Era

    Pioneering zero-lubrication silicon hairsprings and escape wheels impervious to magnetic fields up to 15,000 Gauss.

  • Present Day

    The Architecture Series

    Unveiling Calibre APX-01: integrating space-grade materials with classical hand-finishing for modern connoisseurs.
