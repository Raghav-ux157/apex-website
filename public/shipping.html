Shipping, Warranty & Returns

Our commitment to transparency, transit security, and lifelong horological support.

Every APEX timepiece is transported under complete transit insurance. We partner exclusively with BlueDart Apex Air and DTDC Prime to ensure end-to-end security with OTP delivery verification.

2. 2-Year International Atelier Warranty

Your APEX timepiece is guaranteed against any mechanical defects or material imperfections for a period of twenty-four (24) months from delivery date.

Warranty Scope: ✓ Movement regulation, amplitude irregularities, power reserve failure
✓ Water-resistance seal integrity under specified ATM ratings
✓ Dial indices, hands detachment, or internal crown mechanism failure
✗ Excludes normal wear and tear on leather straps, accidental glass fractures, or unauthorized opening by non-certified technicians.

3. 14-Day Hassle-Free Returns & Exchanges

We want you to hold the timepiece in hand and admire its weight and finishing. If for any reason it does not captivate your wrist, initiate an RMA request from your Client Account or message our Concierge.

  1. Submit return request within 14 days of delivery.
  2. Our courier partner will arrange a complimentary insured doorstep pickup.
  3. Upon inspection at the Mumbai atelier (confirming unworn condition with seals intact), your refund or collection credit is disbursed within 48 business hours.
