SYNTHESIS OF HERITAGE AND CALIBRE

THE JAIPUR LAPIDARY TRADITION MEETS LE LOCLE CHRONOMETRY

The Amber Chrono embodies the soul of the Pink City's centuries-old royal gem-cutting mastery. Each obsidian dial is sliced from single geological specimens, precision-lapped to an unblemished planar tolerance of under 0.2mm, and inlaid with warm rose gold hour batons crafted in the historic quarter of Johari Bazaar.

Underneath the architectural dial beats a Swiss ETA 7750 chronograph movement, re-engineered and tuned across 5 spatial positions within our Jaipur cleanroom facility. The balance bridge is decorated by hand with micro-hammered perlage, and the winding mass is sculpted from solid 22-karat gold filigree reminiscent of the intricate Jali screens of the Hawa Mahal palace.
