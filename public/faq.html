Frequently Answered Questions

Clear insights into our calibres, water resistance ratings, care recommendations, and atelier guarantees.

What movements power APEX timepieces? +
APEX relies on genuine mechanical calibres renowned worldwide for reliability. Our Signature and Sport automatic lines feature authentic Miyota 9000-series and 8000-series Japanese automatic movements operating at 28,800 or 21,600 vph with 40–42 hour power reserves. Our mechanical chronographs use column-wheel Seagull ST19 calibres with exposed exhibition casebacks, while Meridian Slim pieces use ultra-accurate low-profile Miyota quartz engines.
Are APEX watches suitable for both men and women? +
Absolutely. APEX operates on a unisex horological design code. Case diameters range from 36mm to 43mm with ergonomic lug-to-lug proportions that sit gracefully and comfortably on wrists of all sizes. We believe great design has no gender.
How does the 2-Year International Atelier Warranty work? +
Every APEX timepiece includes a serialised metal warranty card. For 24 months from the delivery date, any manufacturing defect, internal movement irregularity, or water seal failure under rated usage is serviced or replaced completely free of charge by our dedicated watchmakers in Mumbai.
Can I shower or swim with my APEX watch? +
Water resistance varies by model. The Terra Diver (20 ATM / 200m) with screw-down crown is fully scuba and swimming certified. Our Chronos X1 and Veloce GMT (10 ATM / 100m) are suitable for swimming and water sports. Dress models (3 ATM to 5 ATM) withstand handwashing, rain, and accidental splashes, but should not be submerged. Never operate chronograph pushers or crowns underwater.
What is your return and exchange policy? +
We offer a 14-day hassle-free return and exchange window. If a timepiece does not suit your wrist or personal aesthetic, you can initiate a return directly from your Client Account portal or by contacting our VIP Concierge. Timepieces must be unworn with protective films intact.
How long does domestic transit take across India? +
All orders ship via BlueDart Express or DTDC Air Express with full transit insurance. Metro destinations (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) typically arrive within 48 to 72 hours. Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities require 3 to 5 business days. You will receive live SMS and WhatsApp tracking as soon as your package leaves the atelier.
